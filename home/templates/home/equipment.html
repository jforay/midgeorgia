Safety & Equipment

Everything you need for a safe and comfortable ride — already on board.

Fully Equipped. Every Time.

Every boat in our fleet is equipped with premium safety gear and modern navigation technology so you can focus on what matters — enjoying your time on the water. No renting equipment, no guessing, no packing — just show up and go.

What's On Every Boat

Depth Finder

Navigate shallow waters with confidence. Our onboard depth finders give you real-time depth readings so you can avoid hazards and find the best fishing spots.

GPS Navigation

Chart your course with confidence. Every boat comes with a GPS navigation system so you always know exactly where you are on the water, no matter how far you roam.

VHF Marine Radio

Stay connected and safe. Our boats feature VHF marine radios for emergency communication, marina coordination, and NOAA weather alerts on the water.

Life Vests

Safety first. We provide USCG-approved life jackets in sizes for every passenger — adults, youth, and children. Your whole family is covered before you leave the dock.

First Aid Kit

Every boat is stocked with a fully equipped first aid kit. Minor cuts, scrapes, and sunburns happen — we make sure you're prepared to handle them right on board.

Bimini Tops

Georgia summers are hot. Our boats feature retractable bimini tops that provide shade and shelter, keeping you cool and comfortable all day long on the water.

Dual Anchors

Two anchors on every boat means you're always prepared to hold your position — whether you're fishing a cove, swimming at a sandbar, or waiting out a passing storm.

Marine Speakers

Bring the playlist. Our boats are outfitted with weather-resistant marine-grade speakers and Bluetooth connectivity so you can soundtrack every moment on the water.

Navigation Lights

Head out early or stay out late. All our boats carry a full set of navigation lights for safe operation in low-light conditions — from dawn fishing runs to evening cruises.

Everything Included. Nothing Extra.

Every piece of safety and navigation equipment is included with your membership — no rental fees, no add-ons. Join Mid Georgia Boat Club and hop on a fully equipped boat anytime you want.
