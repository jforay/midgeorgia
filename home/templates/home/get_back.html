Get Back To What You Love

Life's too short to spend it on maintenance.

We Are Here To Get You Back On The Water

Mid Georgia Boat Club exists for one reason — to put you and the people you love back on the water. Whether it's fishing at sunrise, tubing with the kids, or a quiet cruise at sunset, we make it effortless. No ownership headaches, no waiting, no hassle. Just boating.

Membership Benefits

Full Fleet Access

Choose from our entire fleet of well-maintained boats. Whether you want a pontoon for family outings or a fishing boat for a solo morning run, the right boat is always available for you.

Easy Reservations

Book your boat online 24/7 through our simple member portal. Pick your date, pick your boat, show up. Reservations take minutes, and your spot is secured instantly.

On-Water Training

Never boated before? No problem. Every new member receives comprehensive on-water training from our certified instructors before heading out solo. Safety and confidence guaranteed.

Family Friendly

Every membership covers you and your immediate family. Kids, spouses, parents — your whole crew is welcome aboard. No extra per-trip fees, just time together on the water.

Zero Maintenance

We handle all cleaning, fueling, and maintenance after every trip. You never touch a wrench or a mop. Your only job is to have a great time and return the boat to the dock.

Dedicated Support

Our friendly team is available by phone and right at the marina. Have a question before your trip or need help at the dock? We are always here to make your day on the water exceptional.

More Time For The Moments That Matter

Imagine spending a Saturday morning on the lake with your kids instead of in the driveway doing boat maintenance. Imagine fishing at sunrise without having spent the night before checking the engine, loading the trailer, and hauling across town.

That's the membership difference. We give you back the time, the energy, and the freedom to actually enjoy the water — on your terms, whenever you want, without the burden of ownership.
