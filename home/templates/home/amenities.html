Everything You Need For A Great Day

Mid Georgia Boat Club members get access to far more than just boats. From Kawasaki jet skis to kayaks and paddleboards, we have everything you need to make every trip to the water a memorable one — all included with your membership.

What's Available

Kawasaki Jet Skis

Hit the water at full throttle on our Kawasaki jet skis. Whether you're carving wakes, exploring the shoreline, or just feeling the rush, our jet skis deliver an experience unlike anything else on the lake.

Kayaks

Prefer a slower pace? Our kayaks let you explore quiet coves, fish from the water, or just soak in the scenery at your own speed. Perfect for solo outings or a peaceful trip with a partner.

Paddleboards

Stand-up paddleboarding is one of the best full-body workouts on the water — or just a fun, relaxing way to cruise the lake. Boards are available for all skill levels, beginners included.

Pontoon & Deck Boats

Our full fleet of pontoon and deck boats is ready for family days, sunset cruises, and everything in between. Cleaned, fueled, and stocked with safety gear before every reservation.

Fishing Boats

Lake Sinclair and Lake Oconee are loaded with bass, crappie, and bream. Our dedicated fishing boats come equipped with depth finders and live wells so you can focus on the catch.

Full-Service Marina

Our on-site marina staff is there every day of operation. Dock assistance, fueling, equipment storage, and a clean, professional facility to make your arrival and departure effortless.

The Lake Is Waiting

One membership, endless ways to enjoy the water. Give us a call and find out how easy it is to get started at Mid Georgia Boat Club.
