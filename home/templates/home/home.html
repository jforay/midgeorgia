First Responders & Military

Mid Georgia Boat Club™ proudly offers discounts to all active and retired military and their immediate families. We offer this to all first responders, firemen, police officers, and nurses. Enjoy all the pleasures and excitement of boating without the hassles associated with owning a boat.
