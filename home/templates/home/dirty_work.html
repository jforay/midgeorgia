We Do The Dirty Work

You show up. We handle everything else.

Maintenance-Free Boating

One of the biggest advantages of membership with Mid Georgia Boat Club is that you never have to worry about the work that comes with owning a boat. No cleaning, no fueling runs, no towing, no storage fees, no winterization — we handle all of it so you can focus entirely on making memories on the water.

What We Handle For You

Post-Trip Cleaning

After every outing, our crew washes and cleans the boat inside and out. Sunscreen residue, fish scales, cooler spills — gone. The next member gets a spotless vessel every time.

Fueling

Return to the dock and pay for the fuel you used. That's it. No lugging gas cans, no waiting at the fuel dock yourself — our team handles refueling between every trip.

Routine Maintenance

Oil changes, engine checks, battery maintenance, and all scheduled service are handled by our certified marine technicians. You will never open a surprise repair bill as a member.

Docking & Mooring

Dock lines, fenders, and slip assignments — our marina staff is right there to help you in and out. You do not need to be a docking expert. We've got the slip covered, literally.

Winterization

No hauling out, no engine flushing, no antifreeze headaches. We winterize the entire fleet each season so everything is ready to go the moment warm weather returns.

Storage

No trailer needed. No storage facility fees. No big driveway required. Our boats live at the marina year-round — just show up when you're ready and the boat will be waiting for you.

Your Day on the Water, Step by Step

1

Reserve

Book your boat online through our easy reservation system — anytime, day or night.

2

Arrive

Pull up to the marina. Your boat is already clean, fueled, and waiting right at the dock.

3

Go Boating

Hop on and head out. Fish, cruise, swim, or just enjoy the sunset. It's your day on the water.

4

Return

Bring the boat back to the dock. Pay for the fuel you used. That is your only job.

5

We Handle It

Our crew takes over — cleaning, fueling, inspecting, and docking. See you next time.
