Honoring Those Who Serve

Mid Georgia Boat Club™ is proud to honor the men and women who dedicate their lives to protecting and serving our communities. As a token of our appreciation, we offer exclusive membership discounts to those who serve.

Exclusive Member Discount

We offer a special discounted membership to all active and retired military, first responders, and their immediate families. Enjoy all the pleasures and excitement of boating without the hassles and costs of owning a boat — at a price that honors your service.

Call to Learn More: (239) 745-0110

Who Qualifies?

Active Military

All branches — Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, and Space Force. Immediate family members living in the same household are also eligible for the discount.

Retired Military

Veterans and retired service members from all branches who honorably served our country. Your service doesn't expire — and neither does your discount with us.

Firefighters

Active and retired firefighters from career and volunteer departments alike. You run into burning buildings — you deserve to unwind on the water whenever you want.

Police Officers

Active duty and retired law enforcement officers from local, county, and state agencies. We appreciate your dedication to keeping our communities safe every single day.

First Responders

EMTs, paramedics, and emergency medical technicians. You respond when seconds matter most — let us give you a place to relax and recharge when you're off duty.

Nurses

Registered nurses, licensed practical nurses, nurse practitioners, and other nursing professionals. Your compassion and long hours deserve a well-earned day on the water.

How to Claim Your Discount

It's simple. Bring any one of the following when you come in for your orientation or call us ahead of time to get started:

  • Military ID or DD-214 discharge papers
  • Law enforcement or fire department badge / agency ID
  • Employer verification letter on official letterhead
  • Nursing license or hospital / clinic employee ID

Our team will verify your eligibility and apply your discount to the membership on the spot. Have questions before coming in? Just give us a call — we're happy to walk you through it.

Call (239) 745-0110
